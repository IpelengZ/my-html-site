Nelson Mandela

A symbol of freedom, equality and hope

About Nelson Mandela

Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela was a South African anti-apartheid activist and politician who became the first democratically elected president of South Africa in 1994. He was born on 18 July 1918 in Mvezo, Eastern Cape.

Mandela dedicated much of his life to fighting against apartheid, a system of racial segregation and discrimination in South Africa. Because of his involvement in the struggle for equality, he was imprisoned for 27 years.

After his release from prison in 1990, Mandela continued working towards peace and democracy. He became president in 1994 and encouraged reconciliation between South Africans. He passed away on 5 December 2013, but his legacy continues to inspire people around the world.

Important Moments in His Life

  1. 1918: Born in Mvezo, South Africa.
  2. 1944: Joined the struggle against apartheid through the ANC.
  3. 1964: Sentenced to life imprisonment.
  4. 1990: Released from prison.
  5. 1993: Received the Nobel Peace Prize with F.W. de Klerk.
  6. 1994: Became South Africa's first democratically elected president.
  7. 2013: Passed away at the age of 95.

His Achievements and Legacy

  • Helped lead the fight against apartheid.
  • Became South Africa's first democratically elected president.
  • Received the 1993 Nobel Peace Prize.
  • Promoted equality, human rights and reconciliation.
  • Inspired people around the world to stand against injustice.

Why I Chose to Honour Him

I chose Nelson Mandela because of his courage, determination and commitment to equality. Despite spending many years in prison, he continued to believe in a better future for South Africa. His life reminds us of the importance of forgiveness, standing up for what is right and treating everyone with dignity.
